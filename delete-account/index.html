Delete your account — AI Jump Rope Counter & Tracker

Developer: Murat Can Kuru · Apps: AI Jump Rope Counter & Tracker (Android), Jump Rope Tracker & Counter (iPhone)

Delete in the app

  • Open the app and go to Profile → Settings.
  • Tap Delete account and confirm.

Request deletion by email

  • Send an email to muratcankuruoffical@gmail.com from the address you signed in with.
  • Use the subject Delete my account.
  • We delete your account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your account (Google or Apple sign-in identifier, name and email address).
  • Your cloud backup: workouts, goals and app settings.
  • Your leaderboard entries (display name, country and jump totals).

What is kept

Purchase records stay with Google Play or the App Store and our purchase provider (RevenueCat), which keep them for billing and tax purposes; manage or cancel subscriptions in your Google Play or App Store account. Workouts and recordings stored only on your device are removed when you uninstall the app.
